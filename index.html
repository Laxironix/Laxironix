Crypto glossary

Speak crypto with confidence.

Quick definitions for the terms you'll hear most often.

Solana
A high-speed, low-fee blockchain. $LAX is built on it.
SPL Token
The token standard on Solana, similar to ERC-20 on Ethereum.
pump.fun
A launchpad where new Solana tokens go live and can be bought.
Wallet
An app that stores your crypto keys and lets you sign transactions.
Seed Phrase
The 12–24 word master key to your wallet. Never share it with anyone.
Liquidity
How easily a token can be bought or sold at a stable price.
Market Cap
Price multiplied by circulating supply. A rough size indicator.
DYOR
